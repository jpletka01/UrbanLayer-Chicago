# WS8 — Block Capacity Map (Phase 1)

**Completed:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** Shipped to production in merge commits `d663439` (PR #154, one owner for the capacity rules + the committed commercial building-area artifact), `fef975f` (PR #155, `GET /api/capacity-map`) and `2c4dba7` (PR #156, the map on the Property Profile). The Profile's Build module now has a free fourth map, "How much of what's allowed is built": every parcel within 0.1 mile is colored by existing floor area over what its district's FAR allows (under 25 / 25-50 / 50-80 / 80%+), with a key that names every uncolored state (condo building, part of a multi-parcel complex, split zoning, no allowed FAR, unknown building area, couldn't check) and a line saying how much of the view is colored. A view under 10% colorable is withheld instead of drawn. Districts come from area overlap against the City zoning map, outlines from ptaxsim, building area from the Discovery index else `ingestion/data/commercial_bldg_by_pin.json.gz` (2024 reassessment). Live-verified 2026-10-31: 1601 N Milwaukee 90 of 149 parcels colored (60.4%, standard); the Loop at State & Madison 14 of 138 (10.1%, low); `sweep:live` clean. **Lesson:** measure coverage before drawing (Phase 0's citywide census, then a 200-view random panel at 72.1% colorable pooled), and give every uncolored parcel a named reason so blank never reads as "fully built" or "empty". Known difference kept: the Profile verdict attributes a complex's total area to its keypin while the map does not color that parcel (Jack's decision, on his list).

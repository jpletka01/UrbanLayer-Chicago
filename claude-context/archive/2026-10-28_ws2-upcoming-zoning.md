# WS2 — Upcoming Zoning Signal (no-model phase)

**Completed:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** Shipped to production in merge commits `93a4fea` (PR #118, pending snapshot), `a830cd0` (PR #119, placement and free Profile list) and `9a60e7c` (PR #120, reviewed pending Title 17 text impacts). The daily legislation sync now also keeps a last-good snapshot of the Clerk's non-final zoning and Title 17 text matters (`pending_legislation.db`: lifecycle-collapsed, EDS-free, Address-Points-placed). A Property Profile lists current map filings that name the parcel or sit within 0.5 mile as applicant proposals, and names a pending Title 17 amendment only through a committed, ordinance-read review (`backend/pending_impacts.json`) whose conditions the parcel meets, beside the unchanged current-law figure. Fresh, stale, unavailable, partial and complete/incomplete review are distinct wire and UI states; an unreviewed or outdated review never reads as "none". Live-verified 2026-10-28: 874 zoning matters (591 placed), 48 of 48 pending Title 17 text matters reviewed, `1704 N Milwaukee Ave` shows O2026-0026265 and the M3-3 control shows none. **Lesson:** make "reviewed" a first-class state of the data, tied to the exact record read, so a missing or replaced review is visible instead of silently becoming "nothing relevant". Optional PR4 (page-cited applicant proposal details by local OCR, model fallback off by default) was deliberately not built and stays behind its own accuracy/cost approval.
